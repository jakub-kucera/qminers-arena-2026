# Experiments

Each runs from the repository's root as `uv run python -m experiments.<folder>.<name>` (the tools at the top as
`experiments.<name>`). The Arena's experiments are grouped by what they study:
- **`rounds/`**: the round's workflow: first look, ranking in Rust, paired tests, the policy search, the warm start, the leaderboard, the charts.
- **`tape/`**: reading a tape: the replayable book, its statistics and structure, rebuilding a private tape from the server's logs.
- **`generator/`**: the tape generator recovered from the data: identification, fits per round, calibration against the server's scores, synthetic tapes.
- **`filter/`**: the filter of the hidden price that new orders are placed around, and its settings.
- **`maker/`**: round 1's market-making studies: fills by queue place and book state, the ladder's search, the ceilings.
- **`taker/`**: the forecast taker's studies: forecast quality and gaps, where it earns and loses, its takes and quotes, pretrained forecasts.
- **`audits/`**: round 4's audits of the final and of the round's experiments.
- **`study/`**, **`prosperity/`** and **the tools at the top**: a one-off, the one Prosperity-era module the round
  tools import (the zoo's helpers), and the small tools (PnL segments, the gray-area classifier, ABIDES tapes, the
  IDEAS index).

Those that run on our engine take `--data DIR --days ...`, `--format` its plug-in and `--bot`
(`arena.cli.add_dataset_arguments`); those on the Arena's tapes take the tape's CSV. The Prosperity-era experiments
are in the prep repository, with their numbers in its `IDEAS.md`. Until 9 Nov the Arena's experiments were one
folder, `day/` ("re-run on the day's data"); the commands in the notes written during the event have been
rewritten to the new folders.
